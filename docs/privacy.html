Account and game services

We process your Firebase user ID, sign-in provider and account details supplied by Google sign-in, squad name, profile choices, game progress, currency, items, unlocks, notice acknowledgements and mail claim records. This supports authentication, cloud saves and restoration, duplicate reward prevention, service notices and support. These services require the relevant account and save information.

Purchases and optional ads

For in-app purchases, we verify product IDs, Google Play purchase tokens and purchase states, and retain grant and duplicate-prevention records. Google Play handles payment methods; the game server does not collect card numbers.

When optional rewarded ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, advertising ID, app set ID and other device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are optional. Regional consent choices are provided when applicable; Android settings allow you to reset or delete the advertising ID. Ad-free web and development test rewards are not recorded as real ad views.

Notifications and technical records

If you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes notification tokens and language subscriptions. Notifications can be disabled in game and device settings. App version, operating system, access times, IP addresses and limited error and security records support reliability and fraud prevention. Our error reports exclude authentication tokens, emails and complete save files.

Processing and security

We use Google LLC services: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Google Play and AdMob. Data may be processed on Google infrastructure inside or outside your country. Transfers use HTTPS/TLS encryption; authentication and server rules protect account saves. We do not sell personal data. External services handle data under their own privacy policies and settings. Google Privacy Policy

Retention, deletion and your rights

We retain necessary data while providing account services and delete it after a verified account deletion request or completion of its purpose. Minimum transaction or security records required by law, fraud prevention or a dispute may be retained separately for the necessary period.
